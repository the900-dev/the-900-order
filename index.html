Founding Leadership

Built by Africa’s Next Generation

Founded in 2026 by siblings Zanele Shamain Dube (20) and Siyabonga Bryan Judah (19), The 900 Order was born from a conviction that South Africa and the broader African continent possess unmatched potential.

As young Black South African co-founders, Zanele and Siyabonga established the order to invest directly in themselves and young African founders across the continent—nurturing visionaries with the strategic discipline, systems, and guidance needed to build sustainable enterprises.
